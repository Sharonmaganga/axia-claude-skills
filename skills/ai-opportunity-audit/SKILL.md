# AI Opportunity Audit

Find the 3–5 processes where AI or automation will save the most time or money, and say what to do first.

> AI is only valuable when it's pointed at a real business problem. Start with the work, not the tool.

## 1. Interview (keep it short; ask in batches of 3–4 questions)
- What the organisation does, its sector, team size, and the countries it operates in.
- The tools it already uses (e.g. Excel, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, Odoo, WhatsApp Business, M-Pesa, a core banking system).
- The 5–10 most repetitive or painful tasks: who does each one, how often, and how long it takes.
- Constraints: budget, data sensitivity (customer or member data, health records), internet reliability, and staff skills.

## 2. Score each process (1–5 on each)
| Factor | Question |
|---|---|
| Volume | How often does it happen? |
| Time cost | How many hours a month does it take? |
| Rules-based | Can the steps be written down? |
| Data ready | Is the input digital and structured? |
| Risk | How costly is a mistake? (lower risk = higher score) |

Priority = Volume + Time cost + Rules-based + Data ready + Risk. Show the full table.

## 3. Match a solution to each top process
Choose the simplest option that works, in this order:
1. A spreadsheet formula or template.
2. An off-the-shelf integration (Zapier, Make, n8n).
3. An AI assistant or agent (document processing, chatbot, WhatsApp bot, voice agent).
4. A custom build.

For each, give an estimate of the hours saved per month, the setup effort (S/M/L) and the main risk.

## 4. Deliver a roadmap
- **Quick wins (0–30 days):** low effort, high score.
- **Next (1–3 months).**
- **Later (3–6 months):** bigger builds or anything that needs cleaner data first.
- Add a short "data and governance" note: who owns the data, consent, and compliance with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 or the equivalent law where the organisation operates.

## Rules
- Use estimates the user can check, and show your assumptions.
- Don't recommend a paid tool when a free or existing one works.
- End by offering a clear next step (e.g. pilot the top quick win for 2 weeks and measure the hours saved).
